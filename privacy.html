Privacy, publisher and licences

Written for parents and other grown-ups. In short: Letter Adventures collects nothing about your child.

Who publishes the game

Letter Adventures is published by Sverrhag Consulting AB, Sweden, which is the data controller. Contact: hello@letteradventures.com. Registered office: Lund, Sweden. Company registration number: 559164-9859. VAT number: SE559164985901. Postal address: Pukgränden 7, SE-226 48 Lund, Sweden.

What is saved on the device

The game saves the name or nickname typed on the start screen, the chosen age, language and settings, the stars, stickers, word levels and the alphabet top list in the browser’s local storage, so that your child can carry on where they stopped. This stays on the device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

There are no accounts, no cookies, no advertising, no statistics and no tracking.

What the web host receives

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like every web server it receives the device’s IP address and browser type when pages, pictures and sound clips are fetched, and GitHub states that it logs IP addresses for security purposes. We have no access to those logs. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in making the game available (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). According to GitHub’s privacy statement, the data may be processed in the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

The game contacts no other servers.

Deleting what is saved

Open the settings (the cog) and choose “Delete everything saved”. Clearing the site’s data in the browser does the same.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or erased, and object to its use. Because nothing is sent to us, we normally hold nothing. You may complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se, or to the authority in your own country.

The voices are computer-generated with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech. Typefaces: Fredoka and Andika, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Fredoka, Andika). App icon: the unicorn from Microsoft Fluent Emoji, under the MIT License.
